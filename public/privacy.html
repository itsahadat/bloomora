Bloomora Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Bloomora is a garden match-3 puzzle game made by Md. Sahadat Hosen ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. It applies to the Android app and to the web versions of the game.

Game progress saved on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, boosters and settings are saved on your own device. This data stays on your device unless you turn on cloud save.

Cloud save with Google (Android app, optional)

If you choose "Save with Google", we use Google Firebase Authentication to sign you in. We receive your Google account's user ID, name and email address. Your game progress is then stored in Google Cloud Firestore so you can restore it on another phone. Only your own signed-in account can read or change your save.

You can sign out at any time in the game's Settings. To delete your cloud save and account data, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for in-game rewards. There are no in-app purchases.

  • Android app: ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and app usage information to show and measure ads. Players in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent first. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • CrazyGames: when you play on CrazyGames, ads and saving are handled by CrazyGames under their privacy policy.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore): Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy

Children

Bloomora is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
