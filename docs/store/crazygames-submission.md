## Game details
**Title:** Bloomora

**Category:** Puzzle (tags: Match 3, Casual, Cute, Mouse, Touch)

**Short description:**
Swap fruits and flowers, clear crates and vines, and bloom 100 cozy garden levels.

**Description:**
Bloomora is a cozy garden match-3 puzzle. Swap fruits and flowers to make lines of three or more, clear wooden crates, vines and soil, water thirsty pots and help Bea the bee bring every garden back to bloom.

Match 4 or 5 for line blasts, burst blooms and rainbow bombs, and combine them for huge combos. Use boosters on tricky levels, spin the daily wheel and finish weekly quests. 100 levels across 10 gardens.

**Controls:**
- Mouse: click and drag a tile onto its neighbour to swap.
- Touch: swipe a tile toward its neighbour.
